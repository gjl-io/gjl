## gjl v0.0.1-alpha.4

This alpha refines setup and activity views across Door, Gate, and Vault.

- Redesigns role and Route management around the Listener and Route catalogs.
- Improves Door coding-agent HOME registration cards and profile management.
- Displays Listener ingress activity separately from Route token and API-equivalent price activity.
- Keeps `gjl -v` and `gjl --version` concise while `gjl version` reports executable and protocol metadata.
